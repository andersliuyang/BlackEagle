Review Analysis Guide: Find Product Improvement Opportunities from Negative Reviews

Every negative review is an opportunity for product improvement. With the Review Analyzer tool, you can process hundreds of reviews in minutes and find real improvement directions.

  1. Step 1: Open the product review page
  2. Step 2: Launch the Review Analyzer (AI auto-scrolls to load all reviews)
  3. Step 3: AI batch analyzes and outputs TOP5 positive keywords, TOP5 negative keywords, and user quote excerpts
  4. Step 4: AI gives actionable suggestions for product/description/customer service improvements

Tip: Regular review analysis helps track product reputation trends — catch issues early, fix them fast.
