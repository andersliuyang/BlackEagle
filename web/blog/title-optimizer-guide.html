Title Optimizer Guide: How to Double Your Product CTR with AI

The product title is your first traffic gateway. A great title can bring 30%+ more clicks. Here's how to use the Gild Title Optimizer tool.

  1. Step 1: Open the product edit page and launch the sidebar
  2. Step 2: Click the "Optimize Title" button, or tell the AI "optimize this title"
  3. Step 3: AI reads the current title and analyzes it across 3 dimensions: keyword coverage, selling point delivery, and readability
  4. Step 4: AI generates 10 different title variants, each with an optimization note and estimated impact

Tip: You can specify priorities like "focus on search traffic" or "focus on conversion" — the AI adjusts its strategy.
