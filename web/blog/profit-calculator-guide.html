Profit Calculator: Know Your Margins Before Quoting

Miscalculating costs is the most common reason sellers lose money. The Profit Calculator puts all expenses in one place so you see your true margins before committing.

  1. Step 1: Open the "Profit Calculator" in the sidebar
  2. Step 2: Follow AI prompts to fill in: selling price, cost price, platform commission, shipping, and promotion cost (optional)
  3. Step 3: AI auto-calculates profit amount, profit margin, and break-even volume
  4. Step 4: If margin targets aren't met, AI suggests adjusted pricing

Tip: The "extract from page" mode auto-detects pricing info from the current product page.
