Price Tracking Guide: Auto-Record Competitor Price Changes

Always the last to know when a competitor adjusts prices? The Price Tracking tool auto-detects changes when you browse and notifies you immediately.

  1. Step 1: Open any product page
  2. Step 2: Tell the AI "track this price"
  3. Step 3: When you revisit the product page, AI auto-compares with the last recorded price
  4. Step 4: Price changed? Browser notification pops up + wishlist panel shows price history

Note: Due to MV3 limitations, this is passive detection (triggers when you browse) — no background polling.
