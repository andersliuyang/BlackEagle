Cross-Platform Listing: One Product for Taobao / Pinduoduo / Douyin

Different platforms have different title length limits, detail style preferences, and selling point priorities. The Cross-Platform Adapter lets AI handle the rewriting for you.

  1. Step 1: Open the product page on any platform and launch the sidebar
  2. Step 2: Click "Cross-Platform Adapter" or tell the AI "generate a Taobao version"
  3. Step 3: AI reads all product info and regenerates per target platform rules (word count, keyword density, selling point order)
  4. Step 4: Output includes platform-adapted title, selling points, and detail copy — ready to copy and use

Tip: You can request multiple platform versions at once for batch listing.
